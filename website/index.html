Everything a download manager should do.

And a few things no other one does.

⚡

Two lines, one download

Every file is pulled over Ethernet and Wi-Fi at the same time. The faster line takes more of the work on its own.

🧩

Chrome extension

Click Download in Chrome and DualDL takes over. Right-click any link, or grab videos straight from the page.

📁

Google Drive, properly

Big files, private files and whole shared folders with subfolders. Drive's zip pieces unzip into one folder.

🎬

Video & MP3

YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and 1,000+ sites. Best quality, 1080p, or MP3 with cover art.

🎞️

Ready for Premiere

VP9 and AV1 videos are converted on your Mac's hardware encoder, so they open in Premiere and QuickTime.

🛟

Never starts over

Pause, quit, lose Wi-Fi, even a link that expires: DualDL picks up exactly where it stopped.

🧭

Knows your lines

Shows each connection's ISP and speed, and tells you when both are really the same connection.

🚀

Turbo browsing

Optional: spreads Chrome's tabs over both lines and keeps you online if one ISP drops.

💡Works best with two different internet providers, for example cable from one ISP and Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot from another. If both lines are the same connection, DualDL tells you and uses one.

Grab any video in one click.

Open a video, click the DualDL button, pick the quality. It downloads over both lines and lands as a normal MP4 or MP3.

  • Best quality, 1080p or audio only
  • Uses your Chrome login for members-only videos
  • Files that open everywhere, no codec hunting

Install in a minute.

DualDL isn't on the App Store, so macOS asks once before opening it.

Download & move

Download DualDL.zip, open it, and drag DualDL.app into your Applications folder.

Allow it once

Open DualDL. When macOS warns, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DualDL.app

Add to Chrome

In DualDL go to Settings → Chrome → Show Extension Folder. In chrome://extensions turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick that folder.

🐍If macOS offers to install the Command Line Tools, click Install. DualDL's download engine runs on the Python that comes with them.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. DualDL is open source under the MIT license. No account, no ads, no tracking. The full code is on GitHub.

Will it make my downloads faster?

If your Ethernet and Wi-Fi come from two different providers, a download can use the speed of both added together. If they're the same connection, there's nothing extra to add, and DualDL says so. You still get resume, Drive folders and video downloads.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apple shows that warning for any app that isn't sold through the App Store or notarized with a paid developer account. The code is public, and you can also build it yourself from the source.

Does it work on Intel Macs or Windows?

Not yet. DualDL needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Is my data safe?

Everything runs on your Mac. The Chrome extension only talks to the DualDL app on your own computer, and logins it hands over are sent only to the site they belong to.
